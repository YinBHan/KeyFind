## Shortcut data

The bundled shortcut catalogs are manually curated from the official references
listed in [README.md](README.md). The project stores short descriptions,
keyboard representations, and search terms. It does not bundle source-page
markup or copy the referenced sites. The catalogs are provided for lookup and
may need review when an upstream application changes its shortcuts.
